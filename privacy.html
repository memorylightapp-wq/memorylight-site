Memory Light Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Who we are

Memory Light is made by Ezzulddin Zyara, an independent developer in Toronto, Ontario, Canada ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal information described here. Privacy contact (privacy officer): Ezzulddin Zyara, memorylightapp@gmail.com.

The short version

- Your games, scores, vault items and settings stay on your device.

- Game stats are sent only if you say yes, and you can switch them off any time.

- No ads, no advertising ID, no selling of data, no accounts.

1. What stays on your device

These are saved only in the app's storage on your device: progress, rounds, stars, streaks, checkpoints, settings, language, your Memory Vault items (for example phone numbers, dates and notes you type), memory-test and IQ-game results, Memory Learn progress, Tricks you have seen, and your friend code. We cannot see them. They may be included in your device's own backups (for example iCloud Backup) if you use backups. They are not encrypted by the app, so please do not save passwords, bank PINs, card numbers or ID numbers in the Vault. Deleting the app deletes this data. Text you type in Relax Let Go is never saved or sent.

2. Game stats (only if you agree)

The first time you open the app, it asks whether you want to share game stats. If you say yes, the app sends gameplay events such as: which game, mode or section you used, round reached, stars, mistakes, replays, time taken, test scores, settings chosen, and when a game ended or was left. Each event carries the app version and a session number that is made fresh each time the app opens and is not saved.

Stats are processed by Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC). Google uses a random app-instance identifier created for this installation, basic device information (model, system version, language) and an approximate country worked out from your network (IP) address. Stats do not include your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, advertising ID, Vault contents or anything you type. We use stats only to understand how the games are played and to improve them. They are not sold, not used for advertising and not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites. Stats data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically.

Our legal basis is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time in About → Share game stats. When you turn it off, nothing more is sent and stats still waiting on your device are deleted. Stats already sent stay with Google until they expire, unless you ask us to delete them (see "Your rights").

Google may process this data in the United States and other countries. See Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Firebase privacy information (https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

3. Device features the app uses

- Motion sensors: some Tricks (for example Gravity, Snow Globe, Hologram, Upside Down) read your phone's tilt and shake to move the picture. This is processed instantly on the device and is never saved or sent.

- Notifications: only if you turn on the daily reminder or a Vault timer, the app schedules local notifications on your phone. They are not sent through any server.

- Clipboard: buttons such as "Copy my score" and "Copy" put text on your clipboard. The app never reads your clipboard.

- Sharing: if you tap Invite, your phone's share sheet opens and you choose where the text goes.

4. Emails you send us

If you tap "Send my idea" or write to us, your mail app sends us an email. We receive your email address, your message and the app version. We use them only to read your idea or answer you. Our email is hosted by Google (Gmail). We delete messages we no longer need, at the latest 24 months after the last contact. Please don't include sensitive personal information.

5. Fonts

To show its fonts, the app may download them from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your device's IP address. See https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy.

6. Ratings

The app may show Apple's standard rating request. Ratings and reviews are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

7. Children

Memory Light is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If the player says they are under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/UK), the app does not offer stats sharing. If you believe a child has shared stats, email us and we will delete them.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Canada's PIPEDA, Quebec's Law 25, the EU/UK GDPR, or California law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to withdraw consent, to object, and to complain to a privacy regulator (in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, www.priv.gc.ca; in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information). Because we don't know who you are, we usually can't find your stats unless you give us your stats ID (About → Copy my stats ID). Email us and we will reply within 30 days. We do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising.

9. Security

We keep collection to a minimum, use well-known service providers, and limit who can access the stats (only us). No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

10. Paid plans (future)

If we offer subscriptions, payment is handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. We receive only a record that the subscription is active.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app before the change applies.
