Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

The short version: your game stays on your phone. There are no ads and no accounts. Game stats are shared only if you say yes, and you can switch them off at any time.

Who we are

Memory Light is made by Ezz, an independent app developer in Toronto, Canada. For anything about privacy, write to memorylightapp@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, scores, stars, settings, language, village, bikes and Memory Vault items are saved only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

The Vault is not a password manager. Please do not save passwords, bank PINs, card numbers or ID numbers in it.

Game stats, only if you agree

The first time you open the app, it asks whether you want to share game stats. If you say yes, the app sends simple play information, such as which game you played, the round you reached and how long it took. This helps us understand how the games are played and improve them.

Stats are handled by Google Analytics for Firebase. They include a random ID for this installation, basic device information (model, system version, language) and an approximate country. They do not include your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, advertising ID, Vault items or anything you type.

Stats are not sold and not used for advertising. They are kept for up to 14 months. You can switch stats off at any time in About, under Share game stats.

Things the app uses on your phone

Motion sensors: a few Tricks read your phone's tilt to move the picture. This happens on the device and is never saved or sent.

Notifications: only if you turn on a reminder. They are created on your phone, not sent from a server.

Clipboard: buttons such as Copy put text on your clipboard. The app never reads your clipboard.

Fonts: the app may load its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your device's network address.

If you email us

We receive your email address and your message, and use them only to answer you.

Children

Memory Light is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. If a player says they are under 13 (or under 16 in the EU or UK), the app does not offer stats sharing.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. Because there are no accounts, please include your stats ID (About, then Copy my stats ID) so we can find it. We reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
