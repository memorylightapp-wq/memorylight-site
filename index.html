How it works

Play a little, build a lot

Everything in Memory Light is earned by playing. There is no shop that takes real money.

  1. Play

    Pick a short puzzle: follow a path of lights, type back a code, match a colour, replay a tune. A round takes a minute or two.

  2. Earn sparks

    Every round, lesson and quiet minute earns sparks and light. One player level grows across the whole app.

  3. Build and explore

    Spend sparks on crops, animals, homes and bikes. Your village grows, and the city opens up to ride through.

Lantern Village

A village that is yours

Name your farm and shape it your way. The sparks you earn in the puzzles pay for everything you plant and build.

  • Grow 16 crops and raise animals
  • Bake bread and fill the villagers' orders
  • Place, move and rotate homes and buildings
  • Send your truck with each order into Lantern City

City Ride

Get on a bike and explore

Lantern City is a full 3D city, by day or by night, and it is the same place your village trades with.

  • Deliver parcels and give villagers a lift on the back seat
  • Race the racecourse, the beach promenade and the lighthouse run
  • Find 30 hidden lanterns and the city's secret spots
  • Jump ramps, pull wheelies and chain tricks in the bike park
  • Follow the leader, solve a treasure hunt, join the fun run
  • Unlock a BMX, a road bike, a cargo bike and a tandem with sparks

The Journey

300 levels in 20 glowing worlds

From Lantern Meadow to the Summit of Light. Each level is two or three short puzzles worth up to three stars. Every fifth level opens a treasure chest, and you can share a level code so a friend plays the exact same level.

  • 62 stickers to collect
  • 41 achievements
  • Daily gift calendar
  • Medals for every game
  • Weekly recap card

The puzzles

10 memory games, 100 rounds each

Every game starts gently and grows slowly, with checkpoints, stars and a daily puzzle. Zen mode removes lives and pressure.

  • PathStones light up one by one. Walk the glowing path back.
  • PinA passcode appears on a keypad. Type it back.
  • ColorA shade glows for a moment. Find it among look-alikes.
  • SoundWind chimes play a short tune. Play it back by ear.
  • LocationRemember where each object was.
  • FlashA pattern flashes for a blink. Tap what lit up.
  • PairsOne peek, then match the pairs.
  • MelodyA little piano. Play the tune back.
  • TimeShort and long flashes. Echo the rhythm.
  • BinaryA code of 0s and 1s. Type it back.

Privacy

Your game stays on your phone

No account

There is nothing to sign up for. Your progress, scores, village and vault items are saved on your device.

No ads, no tracking

No advertising, no advertising ID, and no data sold or shared for advertising.

Stats only if you agree

The app asks once whether you want to share anonymous game stats. You can say no, and you can switch it off at any time.
