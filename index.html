Memory Light

A calm world that grows as you play. Solve puzzles to earn sparks, build your own Lantern Village, and ride a bike through Lantern City.

What's inside

  • Lantern Village: a farm and village you build with sparks you earn by playing
  • City Ride: a 3D bike ride through Lantern City, with deliveries, races and hidden secrets
  • The Journey: 300 levels in 20 worlds
  • 10 calm memory games, quizzes, puzzles and relaxing toys

No ads. No account needed. Nothing to buy. Works offline. For iPhone.
