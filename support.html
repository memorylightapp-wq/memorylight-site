Support

How can we help?

Questions, problems or ideas are all welcome. Write to us and you will get a reply by email.

Email memorylightapp@gmail.com

It helps if you tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Getting started

How do I earn sparks?

Play any puzzle, lesson, quiz or relax toy. Every round earns sparks and light. Sparks are what you spend in Lantern Village and in the bike shop.

Where do I find Lantern Village and City Ride?

Lantern Village has its own card in the app. City Ride is inside the village, in Lantern City.

How do I steer the bike?

Use the joystick on the left to steer and pedal. The buttons on the right ring the bell, jump, drift and lift the front wheel.

Your progress

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. There are no accounts and nothing is stored on our servers.

I am changing phones. Can I keep my progress?

Yes. Use the backup code in the app before you switch, then enter it on the new phone. Your device's own backup may also include the app's data.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting the app deletes your progress from the device. Make a backup code first if you want to keep it.

Cost and privacy

Does the app cost anything?

No. There are no ads and nothing to buy. Everything is earned by playing.

Does it need the internet?

No. Memory Light works offline.

What data do you collect?

Nothing, unless you agree to share anonymous game stats. You can switch that off at any time in About, under Share game stats. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I ask for my stats to be deleted?

Email us with your stats ID. You can copy it in About, under Copy my stats ID. We reply within 30 days.

Comfort and safety

Some effects bother my eyes. What can I change?

Open the Comfort settings and switch on Reduce motion or Larger text. The Tricks corner contains optical illusions; please read the Health and safety notice in About before using it.

Is this a medical or brain-training app?

No. Memory Light is for fun and learning. It is not a medical device and makes no claim to improve memory or intelligence outside the games.

Is it suitable for children?

Memory Light is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. It contains no ads, no purchases and no chat.
