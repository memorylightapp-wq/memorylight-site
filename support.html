Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. There are no accounts. Deleting the app deletes your progress, so use the backup code in the app before you change phones.

Does the app cost anything?

No. There are no ads and nothing to buy. Everything is earned by playing.

Does it need the internet?

No. Memory Light works offline.

What data do you collect?

Nothing unless you agree to share anonymous game stats, and you can switch that off at any time. See the Privacy Policy.

Is this a medical or brain-training app?

No. Memory Light is for fun and learning. It is not a medical device and makes no claims about improving memory outside the games.
